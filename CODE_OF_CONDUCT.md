# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas, prompts, workflows, and outputs, not people.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Share evidence when making factual claims.
- Respect copyright, licensing, and platform rules.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Submitting copyrighted material without permission or compatible licensing.
- Encouraging deceptive, misleading, or harmful content practices.
- Presenting speculation as verified fact.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove issues, pull requests, comments, or contributions that violate this code.

Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from participating in the project.

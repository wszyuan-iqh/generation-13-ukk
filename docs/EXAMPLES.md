# Examples

## Sports Event

```text
Use x-algorithm-content-strategist to create a content package for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Target audience:
Chinese-speaking creators and small business owners.

Goal:
Find content and business angles before the event starts.

Requirements:
- verify official dates and host-city facts
- include social sentiment signals
- recommend real-photo cover directions
- write a long-form article draft
- score the final draft
```

# X Algorithm Content Scoring Rubric

Use this reference when a task needs detailed scoring, revision, or final publication checks.

## Topic Matrix

Score each item from 1-5.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain/desire intensity | Mild curiosity | Useful problem | Urgent, costly, emotional, or identity-linked |
| Freshness/timing | Evergreen but stale | Moderately timely | Happening now or clearly upcoming |
| Audience size | Niche | Clear segment | Broad audience or strong niche with high intent |
| Discussion potential | Little to debate | Some opinions | Naturally invites disagreement, stories, or choices |
| Deliverability | Hard to prove | Can explain | Can provide facts, cases, steps, data, or tools |

Topic priority:

- 22-25: publish candidate
- 18-21: refine angle
- 17 or below: hold or discard

## Title Patterns

Use at least five patterns:

- Counterintuitive: `Most people think [A], but [B] is what matters.`
- Benefit: `How [audience] can get [result] from [topic].`
- Mistake: `If you are doing [thing], avoid these [number] mistakes.`
- Breakdown: `Breaking down [case]: why it achieved [result].`
- Comparison: `[A] vs [B]: choose by these [number] signals.`
- Trend: `[Change] is here. The real impact is [specific effect].`
- Checklist: `[Number] things to prepare before [event/action].`
- Postmortem: `After [failure/result], the real lesson is [insight].`

Title score:

- Click desire: 30
- Clarity: 25
- Information density: 20
- Credibility: 15
- Differentiation: 10

Deduct:

- Unclear topic: -20
- Emotion without information: -15
- Unsupported claim: -25
- Cheap hype language: -10

## Cover Image / Thumbnail Score

- Recognition: 30
- Tension or contrast: 25
- Information clarity: 20
- Brand consistency: 15
- Mobile readability: 10

Rules:

- Prefer real photos for real-world events, cities, sports, people, products, and news.
- Use 6-14 characters for Chinese main text when possible.
- Use one visual conflict per image.
- Do not use unlicensed press or social-media photos.
- Do not present AI images as real scenes.

## Article / Thread Structure

Default:

1. Hook
2. Timely context
3. Core thesis
4. Evidence or case
5. 3-7 part framework
6. Practical application
7. Specific interaction question

Body score:

- Hook strength: 20
- Thesis clarity: 20
- Evidence quality: 20
- Actionability: 20
- Rhythm/readability: 10
- Interaction design: 10

## Final 100-Point Publication Score

```text
Recommendation fit =
  topic demand 25
+ freshness/tension/timing 15
+ title click fit 15
+ image stopping power 15
+ body completion value 15
+ interaction trigger 10
+ account consistency 5
- risk deductions
```

Publish guidance:

- 90-100: strong publication candidate
- 85-89: publish after light polish
- 70-84: revise title, image, hook, or evidence
- below 70: rework topic or angle

## Final Checklist

- Facts checked against official or reputable sources when time-sensitive.
- Social-platform claims treated as sentiment unless verified.
- Title promise is fulfilled by the body.
- Cover image is real, licensed, or clearly disclosed if AI-generated.
- Opening gives conflict or value within three seconds.
- Body contains concrete examples, facts, steps, or templates.
- Ending asks one specific question, not a generic "what do you think?"
- Output includes platform-specific adaptations when useful.
